## Description (paste into Payhip)

Most people who pick up a guitar quit in the first few weeks. Not because they can't do it — because every beginner book front-loads boring theory, note-reading, and finger drills before you ever play something that sounds like music.

The First-Riff Method is built around the opposite idea: **get you sounding good immediately, then keep you hooked long enough to actually get good.**

You'll play a recognizable riff in the first five minutes — one finger, zero theory. From there, nine short stages carry you from total beginner to strumming real songs and playing your first solo. Every stage ends in something you can actually play. It's the anti-quit guitar method.

And it's genuinely interactive — not a PDF:

- 🎸 A built-in chromatic tuner — step one isn't "go buy a tuner"
- 🔁 A 60-second chord-change game that turns the most important beginner skill into a high score you keep beating
- 🥁 A live strum trainer with a real metronome
- 🎶 A tap-to-hear chord explorer and a playable pentatonic fretboard you actually solo on
- 📈 A progress tracker that fills as you go

**What's inside — 9 stages:**
0. The First Riff — sound like music in 5 minutes
1. Power chords & the palm mute
2. Your first chord change (the 60-second game)
3. The four chords behind most pop songs
4. Strumming & feel — the live metronome
5. The capo cheat — every key, same easy shapes
6. The barre chord, finally made easy
7. Your first solo — a playable pentatonic box
8. Make it yours — jam, write, play by ear
…plus the chromatic tuner, yours forever.

**Who it's for:** total beginners, anyone who's tried and quit, adults finally doing it, parents teaching a kid. If you can press a string and tap a button, you can start.
